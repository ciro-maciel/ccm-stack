# 10 · Review before you show

**Module 2 · Prove** · 15 minutes · [Previous](09-map-every-feature.md) · [Next: Screens that respect people](11-screens-that-respect-people.md) · Skill: [`critique`](../skills/critique/SKILL.md)

## The mistake

The agent says the new settings page is done. You open it: a button sits on the card's edge, a deleted item loads forever, and a long name overflows on the phone. Each took you seconds to see, and the agent saw none of them.

## The rule

Before anyone sees a screen you changed, capture it in every state, theme and size, score it on five axes, and fix whatever scores under 7.

## Make it stick

The `critique` skill defines the captures (light and dark, desktop and phone; loading, empty, error, populated and edge) and the five axes (coherence, hierarchy, detail, function, distinction). The report has three lists: kept, fixed, still open.

## Try it

After your next UI change:

```
Use critique on the screens you changed. Show me the captures and the scores before and after your fixes.
```

## Checkpoint

The last screen you shipped has captures of all five states, and its lowest score is 7 or more.
